X-ray Lens

Upload a chest or bone X-ray for an experimental ML analysis. This is not a medical diagnosis.

How it works

  1. Checks whether the upload resembles an X-ray.
  2. Routes it to chest or bone.
  3. Runs a body-part-specific abnormality model.
  4. Shows model confidence and a plain-language explanation.
